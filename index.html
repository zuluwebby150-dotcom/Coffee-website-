Our story

Good coffee takes time. We never rush it.

Amber & Oak started with a second-hand roaster and a simple idea: coffee should feel like being welcomed home. Today we still roast in small batches, bake before sunrise and learn our regulars' orders by heart.

  • 🌱
    Ethically sourcedSingle-origin beans from growers we know by name.
  • 🔥
    Roasted in small batchesFresh every week, never sitting on a shelf.
  • 🤍
    Made for communityA place to work, catch up or simply breathe.
